# Folklore waiting hints

The catalog contains one short entry per distinct subject. Traditional beliefs and modern Internet stories are
presented as folklore or fiction, not as claims that the creatures exist. Movie-only and modern literary
inventions are excluded; SCP fiction and Internet-born legends are included.

## World folklore (`world.json`)

Short, edited English descriptions and original source-based descriptions, adapted from the linked Wikipedia
articles. Copyright belongs to their respective Wikipedia contributors; editorial contributions copyright 2026
Ryadel. This data file is licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Each entry supplies its source article and the revision used. Edits remove pronunciation guides, parentheses and
references, shorten sentences, or replace an excerpt with an original concise description of the same subject.
Article histories, linked from each source, identify the contributors.

## SCP fiction (`scp.json`)

Original concise descriptions adapted from the individual SCP Wiki articles linked in the data. Each entry credits its authors.
Copyright belongs to those authors; editorial contributions copyright 2026 Ryadel. This data file and its adapted
text are licensed under [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
The adaptations summarize the creatures' appearance, behavior or fictional containment premise.
No SCP images, logos or audio are included.

See the [SCP licensing guide](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/licensing-guide) and
[Wikipedia reuse guidance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Reusing_Wikipedia_content).
